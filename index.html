Approach

How I approach product work

  1. 01Start with the people and their contextIn rural India, many farmers and workers have worn fingerprints, so fingerprint checks at village banking points often failed. I pushed banks and agents to adopt face authentication instead.
  2. 02Understand the whole system before changing itA UPI payment involves two banks, two payment providers, NPCI and the regulator. For BioAuth I mapped risk, liability and incentives for each before we chose an on-device design, and set rules for edge cases such as a user changing phone or SIM.
  3. 03Measure, then fix the causesAt Fino I tracked failed transactions by cause and fixed them with partners and support teams. Transaction success rose from 96% to 98%.
