A little more about me

I trained as a mechanical engineer and moved into product in 2018 at Fino Payments Bank. Since then I’ve worked on payments for very different users: shopkeepers acting as banking agents in villages, travellers paying abroad, and people using digital payments for the first time.

Most of that work was at NPCI, where a product change needs agreement from banks, apps and regulators before it ships. I enjoy that kind of problem, and I still like getting into the technical detail myself.

I’m now doing an MBA at Cornell Johnson in New York, where I co-founded the Cornell FinTech Club. I’ve spoken at Global Fintech Fest (2023) on UPI’s global opportunity.

Outside work

I’m a certified paraglider pilot and enjoy high-altitude treks, Formula 1, and old stepwells and forts.
