Layer Up NYC

After moving to New York, I kept dressing for the morning forecast and regretting it by evening. Layer Up plans what to wear from the hourly weather, how I’m getting around and how much cold, heat and rain I can take.

Open Layer UpView the code on GitHub
Background
Weather apps show the forecast, not what to wear, and they treat walking outside, the subway and a classroom the same.
Hypothesis
Combining “feels like” temperature, how you travel and personal tolerance gives more useful advice than a forecast alone.
Key decision
Rules choose the outfit so identical inputs give identical results and every rule can be tested. AI is reserved for reading free-text input.
Implementation
Plain HTML and JavaScript on GitHub Pages; Open-Meteo for weather; Photon for place search; device location where permitted.
Testing and validation
63 unit tests, 28 browser checks, and an outside review that found 2 issues, both fixed.
Next steps
Planned, not built: sync across devices, a daily “layer up or down” message, and an AI chat protected by a passkey.

Product decisions and trade-offs

Place search

Searching for landmarks

A search for “Times Square” matched a town called Time in Norway. I switched to Photon, which finds landmarks and ranks results near you.

Reliability

Rules relax instead of freezing

A review found an endless loop when the wardrobe couldn’t meet every rule. The rules now relax one step at a time, and the app says what’s missing.

Security · planned

The AI key stays on a server

The AI key will stay on a server, never on the phone; a passkey will unlock the chat.
