Layer Up NYC

After moving to New York, I kept dressing for the forecast and regretting it a few hours later. Layer Up plans what to wear from the weather, how I'm getting around, and how much cold, heat and rain I can take.

Open Layer Up View the code on GitHub
The problem

Weather apps tell you the forecast, not what to wear. They also ignore the difference between walking outside, taking the subway, sitting in class and going for a run.

The bet

My hypothesis: combining "feels like" temperature, how you travel and your tolerance for the weather gives more useful outfit advice than a generic forecast.

The call

Rules decide the outfit; AI will only interpret free text.

I rejected letting AI choose the outfit because I wanted the same inputs to give the same result, and every rule to be testable.

Built

Decisions I'd defend in an interview

Place search

Switched geocoders after "Time" returned Norway

The first geocoder treated "Time" as a town in Norway and missed landmarks. Photon finds parks and landmarks and ranks results near you.

Reliability

Rules relax instead of freezing

An outside review exposed an endless loop when the wardrobe couldn't meet the rules. Now the rules relax one step at a time and the app says what's missing.

Security

The AI key will stay on the server

Planned: the key lives on the server, never on the phone, and a passkey will unlock the AI chat.
